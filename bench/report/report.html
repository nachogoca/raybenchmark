Method and caveats

Serving containers are pinned to cores 0–5 and the load generator to 6–7 (this CPU has hyperthread siblings). Capacities come from short search steps, one repeat, and land on a ×1.5 rate ladder, so neighbouring values are one step apart. Run-to-run noise on this laptop is about ±3 ms. Results are a laptop-CPU snapshot, not cloud numbers.
